About Eduvana

Language learning, made steady and realistic.

Eduvana is an independent education site for adults teaching themselves a new language.

We focus on what helps self-learners keep going: clear goals, honest time estimates, vocabulary methods that work and simple daily routines. We do not sell courses or lessons.

Grounded

We draw on widely used frameworks such as the CEFR and well-studied techniques like spaced review.

Honest

We avoid promises of fluency in weeks, and say so when estimates vary.

Reviewed

Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated.
